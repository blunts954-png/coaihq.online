========================================
PRICING PSYCHOLOGY
========================================

DO:
- Frame upsells as "insurance against mistakes" (e.g., "Make sure you don't miss procedural deadlines")
- Use social proof ("What most people do...")
- Offer free option FIRST (builds trust)
- Be honest about weak cases (builds credibility for future upsells)

DON'T:
- Pressure people with weak cases to pay
- Use scarcity tactics ("Only 3 spots left!")
- Oversell the Premium Package (it's just templates)
- Hide the free option
