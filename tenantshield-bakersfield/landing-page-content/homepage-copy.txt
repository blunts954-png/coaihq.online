FAQ SECTION
========================================

Section Headline:
Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Is this legal advice?
A: No. TenantShield provides educational analysis based on California tenant law. This is NOT legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship. For legal advice specific to your situation, consult a licensed California attorney.

Q2: How long does the analysis take?
A: 2-5 minutes. You'll receive your case analysis via email almost immediately after submitting the form.

Q3: How much does it cost?
A: Case analysis is FREE. Connecting with a local advocate for a free 15-minute consultation is FREE. Optional upgrades:
   - Premium Letter Package (professionally formatted letters): $29
   - Extended paralegal consultation (10 minutes): $15

Q4: Who are the advocates you connect me with?
A: Licensed paralegals and tenant rights advocates based in Bakersfield. They specialize in California tenant law. You're under no obligation to hire them—the initial 15-minute consult is free.

Q5: What if my case is weak?
A: We'll tell you honestly. If you don't have a strong legal position, we won't encourage you to waste time or money pursuing it. However, we'll still connect you with an advocate for a second opinion—sometimes small details matter.

Q6: Can you represent me in court?
A: No. TenantShield is an educational platform, not a law firm. However, the advocates we connect you with may be able to help you prepare for small claims court or refer you to an attorney if needed.

Q7: Is my information private?
A: Yes. We never share your information with anyone except the advocate you're connected with (and only with your consent). See our privacy policy for details.

Q8: What types of cases do you handle?
A: We specialize in common California tenant issues:
   - Security deposit disputes
   - Eviction defense
   - Uninhabitable conditions (mold, pests, broken appliances)
   - Illegal rent increases
   - Retaliation
   - Privacy violations

   If your issue is complex (e.g., discrimination, domestic violence), we'll refer you to specialized resources.

Q9: How do I know if I should trust this AI analysis?
A: Our AI is based on California Civil Code and case law. However, AI can't replace a lawyer. That's why we connect you with a real paralegal or advocate for a free consultation—they'll confirm whether the AI analysis is accurate for your specific situation.

Q10: What if I've already talked to a lawyer and they said I have no case?
A: You may not have a case, or you may have talked to the wrong lawyer. Tenant law is very specific, and not all attorneys specialize in it. Get a second opinion from a tenant rights advocate (free consultation). If they agree you have no case, you'll know for sure.
