# PROVIDER RESEARCH GUIDE
How to Find & Vet Bakersfield Tenant Advocates

## OVERVIEW

**Goal:** Find 5-10 qualified Bakersfield paralegals and tenant advocates to partner with

**Time required:** 30-60 minutes

**What you're looking for:**
- Licensed paralegals or tenant rights attorneys
- Based in Bakersfield or serving Kern County
- Specialize in landlord-tenant law
- Good online reviews (4+ stars)
- Active practice (responding to inquiries)

---

## SEARCH METHODS

### **Method 1: LinkedIn (Best for Paralegals)**

**Search query:** "paralegal Bakersfield"

**How to search:**
1. Go to LinkedIn.com
2. Type "paralegal Bakersfield" in search bar
3. Filter: People → Location: Bakersfield, CA

**What to look for:**
- Current position: Paralegal (not unemployed)
- Specialization: Tenant rights, landlord-tenant law, housing law
- Activity: Posted recently (shows they're active)

**How to contact:**
- Send connection request with note: "Hi [Name], I'm launching a tenant rights platform in Bakersfield and wanted to connect."
- After they accept: Send pitch DM (use template from provider-pitch-email.txt)

**Expected results:** 10-20 paralegals in Bakersfield area

---

### **Method 2: Google Search (Best for Attorneys)**

**Search queries:**
- "Bakersfield tenant rights attorney"
- "landlord tenant lawyer Bakersfield"
- "eviction defense attorney Bakersfield"
- "housing attorney Kern County"

**How to search:**
1. Google each query
2. Click on law firm websites (skip ads for now)
3. Look for "Practice Areas" → Landlord-Tenant Law

**What to look for:**
- Specialize in tenant representation (not landlord representation)
- Offer consultations (shows they're open to new clients)
- Contact info visible (email, phone)

**How to contact:**
- Email using contact form or direct email
- Use template from provider-pitch-email.txt

**Expected results:** 5-10 attorneys/law firms

---

### **Method 3: Legal Aid Organizations**

**Search queries:**
- "Bakersfield legal aid"
- "Kern County legal services"
- "California Rural Legal Assistance"

**Organizations to research:**
1. **Greater Bakersfield Legal Assistance (GBLA)**
   - Website: https://www.gbla.org
   - Focus: Low-income tenants
   - Contact: info@gbla.org

2. **California Rural Legal Assistance (CRLA)**
   - Website: https://www.crla.org
   - Kern County office: (661) 325-5943
   - Focus: Farmworkers, low-income residents

3. **Legal Services of Northern California**
   - May serve Kern County (check coverage area)

4. **Kern County Bar Association**
   - Website: https://www.kernbar.org
   - Lawyer Referral Service (may have tenant attorneys)

**How to contact:**
- Call or email directly
- Introduce as partnership opportunity (not solicitation)
- Emphasize you're helping their mission (educating low-income tenants)

**Expected results:** 2-3 legal aid organizations

---

### **Method 4: Yelp (Best for Reviews & Reputation)**

**Search query:** "tenant attorney Bakersfield"

**How to search:**
1. Go to Yelp.com
2. Search "tenant attorney" or "eviction lawyer"
3. Filter: Location: Bakersfield, CA
4. Sort by: Rating (highest first)

**What to look for:**
- 4+ star rating (minimum)
- 5+ reviews (shows they're established)
- Positive reviews mentioning tenant representation
- Recent activity (responded to reviews in last 6 months)

**Red flags (avoid these):**
- Mostly landlord representation (conflict of interest)
- Bad reviews about communication or responsiveness
- No reviews (too new or inactive)

**How to contact:**
- Call phone number listed
- Or send message via Yelp

**Expected results:** 3-5 highly-rated attorneys

---

### **Method 5: State Bar Directory (Official Source)**

**Search query:** California State Bar Lawyer Search

**How to search:**
1. Go to: https://apps.calbar.ca.gov/attorney/LicenseeSearch/QuickSearch
2. Search by:
   - Location: Bakersfield, CA
   - Practice area: Landlord-Tenant, Housing Law
3. Filter: Active (licensed)

**What to look for:**
- Active license (not suspended or disbarred)
- Good standing (no disciplinary actions)
- Practice area matches (landlord-tenant, housing)

**How to contact:**
- Use contact info from State Bar profile
- Or search their name + "Bakersfield" to find law firm website

**Expected results:** 10-20 licensed attorneys

---

### **Method 6: Facebook Groups (Local Community)**

**Search query:** "Bakersfield renters" or "Bakersfield community"

**How to search:**
1. Facebook → Groups
2. Search "Bakersfield"
3. Join 3-5 groups with 1,000+ members

**What to look for:**
- Posts about landlord issues (shows demand)
- Comments recommending specific attorneys (social proof)
- Local advocates posting helpful advice

**How to contact:**
- Search for attorney names mentioned in comments
- Google them to find contact info
- Send pitch email

**Expected results:** 2-3 names recommended by community

---

### **Method 7: Nextdoor (Hyper-Local)**

**How to search:**
1. Sign up for Nextdoor (if not already)
2. Join Bakersfield neighborhoods
3. Search "landlord" or "tenant" in posts

**What to look for:**
- Neighbors asking for attorney recommendations
- Responses with specific names (social proof)

**How to contact:**
- Search attorney name on Google
- Send pitch email

**Expected results:** 1-2 highly recommended local advocates

## QUALITY OVER QUANTITY

**Better to have:**
- 3 great providers who respond quickly and close deals
- Than 10 mediocre providers who ghost your leads

**Focus on:**
- Responsiveness (reply within 24 hours)
- Specialization (tenant law, not general practice)
- Reputation (good reviews, trusted in community)
- Alignment (care about helping tenants, not just making money)

---

## OUTREACH TIMING

**Best time to reach out:**
- **Email:** Tuesday-Thursday, 9 AM - 11 AM (most likely to be read)
- **Phone:** Wednesday-Thursday, 10 AM - 2 PM (less busy)
- **LinkedIn:** Evenings or weekends (when people check personal accounts)

**Avoid:**
- Monday mornings (everyone's swamped)
- Friday afternoons (weekend mode)
- Lunch hours (11:30 AM - 1:30 PM)

## FOLLOW-UP SCHEDULE

**If no response:**

- **Day 1:** Send initial pitch email
- **Day 3:** Follow-up email (bump to top of inbox)
- **Day 5:** Final follow-up ("Last email from me—still interested?")
- **Day 7:** Move on to next provider

**Don't waste time chasing ghosts.** If they don't respond in 5 days, they're not interested.

---

## RED FLAGS (AVOID THESE PROVIDERS)

❌ **Primarily represents landlords** (conflict of interest)
❌ **Bad online reviews** (will hurt your brand)
❌ **Unlicensed or suspended** (legal liability)
❌ **Requests payment upfront** (before seeing lead quality)
❌ **Rude or dismissive in first interaction** (will be rude to your users)
❌ **No website or online presence** (unprofessional)

**Trust your gut.** If something feels off, move to the next provider.
