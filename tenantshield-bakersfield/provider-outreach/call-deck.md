# PROVIDER CALL DECK
Script & Slides for Provider Pitch Calls

## OVERVIEW

**Call duration:** 15 minutes
**Objective:** Get provider to agree to test batch (5 free leads)
**Preparation:** Review provider's background, have sample cases ready

---

## PRE-CALL CHECKLIST

Before the call:
- □ Researched provider (LinkedIn, website, reviews)
- □ Identified their specialty (evictions, deposits, general tenant law)
- □ Prepared 2-3 sample cases to share
- □ Calendar link ready (for follow-up if needed)
- □ Note-taking doc open (track objections, preferences)

---

## CALL STRUCTURE (15 MIN)

### **1. OPENING (0:00-2:00) - Build Rapport**

**Script:**

> "Hey [NAME], thanks for hopping on! I'll keep this to 15 minutes—I know you're busy.
>
> Quick question before we start: How do you typically get new tenant cases? Referrals, walk-ins, online?"

**Why ask this:**
- Shows you care about their current process
- Identifies their pain points (e.g., "referrals are slow")
- Warms them up before the pitch

**Listen for:**
- If they say "I'm swamped" → position as "future pipeline"
- If they say "slow right now" → position as "immediate leads"
- If they say "mostly referrals" → position as "consistent lead flow"

---

### **2. THE PITCH (2:00-7:00) - Explain TenantShield**

**Script:**

> "Got it. So here's what I'm building:
>
> **TenantShield** is a platform that helps Bakersfield tenants understand their rights using AI. People Google things like 'landlord won't return my deposit' or 'eviction notice Bakersfield,' land on our site, and get instant case analysis.
>
> Here's the flow:
> 1. User fills out a form describing their landlord issue
> 2. Our AI analyzes it against California law—Civil Code 1950.7, AB 1482, habitability statutes, etc.
> 3. We give them a strength rating: STRONG, MODERATE, or WEAK
> 4. We send them a detailed action plan + draft letters
> 5. We connect them with you for a free 15-minute consult
>
> Your part is simple:
> - Take the free consult (which you probably already offer anyway)
> - Decide if it's a case you want to help with
> - Let me know if they convert to a paying client
>
> The key difference: **These aren't random people asking 'do I have a case?' These are people who already know their case strength, have draft letters, and just need expert confirmation.**
>
> Less hand-holding. More qualified."

**Visual (if screen-sharing):**
- Show homepage mockup or live site
- Show sample AI analysis output
- Highlight: "User gets this BEFORE talking to you"

---

### **3. SAMPLE CASES (7:00-10:00) - Prove Lead Quality**

**Script:**

> "Let me show you a real example from our test group.
>
> [Read Sample Case 1 - Security Deposit from sample-cases.md]
>
> This is what you'd receive: user's info, our AI analysis, case strength rating, and what we already told them to do.
>
> Notice:
> - They have evidence (photos, texts)
> - They know the statute (§ 1950.7)
> - They've already been told the next steps
> - They're ready to move (not just 'thinking about it')
>
> These are the leads you'll get. Not tire-kickers. Not people with no case."

**If they seem skeptical:**

> "I get it—you've probably been burned by 'lead generation' companies before. That's why the first 5 leads are FREE. No risk for you. If they're garbage, we part ways. If they're good, we keep going."

---

### **4. THE DEAL (10:00-12:00) - Present Offer**

**Script:**

> "Here's how this works:
>
> **TEST BATCH (FREE):**
> - I send you 5 qualified leads over the next 1-2 weeks
> - You take the free consults
> - You decide which cases to help with
> - Zero cost to you
>
> **AFTER THAT (IF YOU LIKE IT):**
> You pick one of these options:
> - **Option 1:** $30 per lead (flat fee, pay weekly)
> - **Option 2:** 20% of fees you collect (revenue share—you only pay if you make money)
> - **Option 3:** Monthly retainer for exclusive Bakersfield access ($200-$500/month depending on volume)
>
> Which model sounds most appealing to you?"

**Why offer 3 options:**
- Different providers prefer different models
- Revenue share = lower risk (appeals to cautious providers)
- Flat fee = predictable (appeals to busy providers)
- Retainer = exclusive (appeals to competitive providers)

**Listen for:**
- If they say "I like revenue share" → they're risk-averse but interested
- If they say "flat fee is fine" → they're confident in conversion rates
- If they say "exclusive sounds good" → they don't want competition

---

### **5. OBJECTION HANDLING (12:00-14:00) - Address Concerns**

**Be ready for these:**

| **Objection** | **Response** |
|---------------|--------------|
| "I don't have time." | "That's why we pre-screen. You're just doing what you already do—free consults. The first 5 are free, so if it's not worth your time, we stop." |
| "Are these actually qualified?" | "Great question. That's why I'm offering a test batch. Let me send you 1-2 this week and you tell me if they're qualified." |
| "What if they're all weak cases?" | "We filter out weak cases. You'll only get STRONG and MODERATE. But if you disagree with our assessment, we adjust the filter." |
| "How many leads per week?" | "Starting with 5-10 per week. If you want more (or less), we can adjust based on your capacity." |
| "What's the catch?" | "No catch. I make money when you make money. If I send you junk, you won't work with me. Simple." |

**If they're STILL hesitant:**

> "Here's what I propose: Let me send you 1 lead THIS WEEK. Just one. You take the consult. If it's a good lead, we continue. If it's garbage, I'll leave you alone. Deal?"

---

### **6. CLOSING (14:00-15:00) - Get Commitment**

**Script:**

> "Alright, here's what I'm hearing: [SUMMARIZE THEIR MAIN CONCERN OR INTEREST].
>
> So let's do this:
> - I'll send you [1-5] leads starting [THIS WEEK / NEXT WEEK]
> - You take the free consults
> - We debrief on Friday to see how it went
>
> Sound good?"

**If YES:**

> "Awesome. Last thing: What types of cases do you prefer?
> - Security deposits?
> - Evictions?
> - Habitability?
> - All of the above?
>
> I'll prioritize those for you."

**Get their preferences:**
- Case types (deposits, evictions, rent increases, etc.)
- Contact method (email, text, phone)
- Availability (weekdays, evenings, weekends)

**End with:**

> "Perfect. You'll get an email from me [TODAY / TOMORROW] with the first lead. Talk soon!"

**If NO / MAYBE:**

> "No problem. If things change or you get less busy, feel free to reach out. I'll keep you on my list for future opportunities."

**Don't push.** Move on to the next provider.

## POST-CALL CHECKLIST

After the call:
- □ Send follow-up email within 2 hours (recap agreement)
- □ Add provider to tracking spreadsheet (name, preferences, deal terms)
- □ Send first lead within 24-48 hours (if they agreed)
- □ Set reminder to debrief after 5 leads sent

## CALL METRICS TO TRACK

After 5-10 calls, analyze:

- **Call-to-Agreement Rate:** How many said "yes" to test batch?
- **Common Objections:** What did most providers worry about?
- **Preferred Deal Model:** Flat fee vs. rev share vs. retainer?
- **Preferred Case Types:** Deposits, evictions, habitability?

Use this data to refine your pitch.

---

## RED FLAGS (BAD PROVIDERS)

Don't work with providers who:
- ❌ Demand payment upfront (before seeing lead quality)
- ❌ Refuse to offer free consults (shows they're not user-friendly)
- ❌ Are rude or dismissive on the call (will be rude to your users)
- ❌ Don't specialize in tenant law (general practitioners are weaker)
- ❌ Have bad online reviews (Google them first!)

**Better to have 1 great provider than 3 mediocre ones.**

---

## GOAL FOR WEEK 1

- **5 provider calls scheduled**
- **2-3 providers agree to test batch**
- **1 provider receives first lead by end of week**

If you hit this, you're on track.

---

## CONFIDENCE BOOSTERS

If you're nervous about calls:

1. **Practice:** Do 1-2 practice calls with a friend
2. **Record:** Record your first call (with permission) and listen back
3. **Iterate:** Each call gets easier
4. **Remember:** They need you as much as you need them (legal services is a referral-driven business)

**You're offering them qualified leads. That's valuable. Don't undersell it.**
